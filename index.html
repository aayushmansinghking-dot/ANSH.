Father of the Nation

Mahatma Gandhi was the moral backbone of India’s freedom struggle. He transformed political resistance into a mass movement based on truth, courage, and non-violence. His life itself became his message.

Early Life & Education

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. Raised in a spiritual household, he learned discipline and honesty early in life. He studied law in London and later worked in South Africa, where injustice shaped his philosophy of Satyagraha.

Role in Freedom Movement

Gandhi led India through non-violent resistance movements like the Non-Cooperation Movement, Dandi March, and Quit India Movement. He united millions and proved that moral strength can defeat oppression.

Historic Timeline

1869 – Birth in Porbandar
1893 – South Africa struggle
1915 – Return to India
1930 – Dandi March
1942 – Quit India Movement
1947 – Independence
1948 – Martyrdom

Famous Slogans & Quotes

“Be the change that you wish to see in the world.”

“Truth is God.”

“An eye for an eye makes the whole world blind.”

Gandhian Values

Gandhi believed in Ahimsa (Non-Violence), Satya (Truth), self-reliance, simplicity, and unity. His philosophy continues to guide humanity toward peace and justice.
