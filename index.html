Mahatma Gandhi

Father of the Nation – India 🇮🇳

Mahatma Gandhi led India to freedom through truth, sacrifice and non-violence.

Biography

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. Influenced by his mother Putlibai, he grew into a disciplined and moral individual.

After studying law in London, Gandhi went to South Africa where racial injustice transformed him into a leader of peaceful resistance.

Returning to India, he became the soul of India’s freedom movement using non-violence as a weapon.

Personal Details

  • Born: 2 October 1869
  • Parents: Karamchand Gandhi & Putlibai
  • Wife: Kasturba Gandhi
  • Profession: Lawyer, Leader
  • Ideology: Truth & Non-Violence
  • Died: 30 January 1948
