Father of the Nation

Mahatma Gandhi led India’s freedom struggle through truth and non-violence. His life proved that moral power is stronger than weapons.

Early Life

Born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gandhi grew up with strong values. He studied law in London and later faced racial injustice in South Africa, shaping his philosophy of Satyagraha.

Freedom Struggle

Gandhi led movements like Non-Cooperation, Dandi March, and Quit India. He united millions without violence and changed world history.

Historic Timeline

1869 – Birth
1893 – South Africa
1915 – Return to India
1930 – Dandi March
1942 – Quit India
1947 – Independence
1948 – Martyrdom

Famous Quotes

“Be the change you wish to see.”

“Truth is God.”

“An eye for an eye makes the whole world blind.”

Gandhian Values

Ahimsa, Truth, Simplicity, Self-discipline and Unity — values that still guide humanity.
